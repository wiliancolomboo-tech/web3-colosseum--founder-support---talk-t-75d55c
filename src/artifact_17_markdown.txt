2/3 How CULP works:
✍️ Founders & Users dual-sign interview attestations directly via Solana PDAs.
🔐 Feedback notes are hashed (SHA-256) for data privacy while preserving verifiable integrity.
⚡ Reaching 5 unique users unlocks an on-chain milestone event triggering real-time @resend emails & @Slack alerts!
